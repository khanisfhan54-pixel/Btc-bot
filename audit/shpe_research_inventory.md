## Current SHPE labels

- Schema version: `shpe-labels.v1.0.0`.
- Positive label: future bar within the 3-bar horizon sweeps the prior 20-bar high or low pool by 1 bps and closes back through the pool.
- Negative label: full horizon exists and no positive sweep occurs.
- Ignored rows: insufficient 20-bar past pool window or insufficient 3-bar future horizon.
- Positive labels carry `event_timestamp_ms`; negative labels leave `event_timestamp_ms` null.
